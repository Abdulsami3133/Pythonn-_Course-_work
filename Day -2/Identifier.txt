IDENTIFIERS:

Rules for Identifiers >

1.Must start with a letter or underscore (_).
2.Can contain letters, digits, and underscores.
3.Cannot start with a digit.
4.Cannot contain spaces or special characters (@, #, $, -, etc.).
5.Cannot be a keyword (e.g., if, class, while).
6.Identifiers are case-sensitive in languages like Python, Java, and C (age, Age, and AGE are different).

VARIABLES:

Rules for Variable Names >
1.Must start with a letter or underscore (_).
2.Can contain letters, digits, and underscores.
3.Cannot start with a digit.
4.Cannot contain spaces or special characters.
5.Cannot be a keyword.
6.Are case-sensitive (age, Age, and AGE are different).
